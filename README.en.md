### Five Ws

| Question | Velocity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Who? | Learners, students and people working with AI. Created by [SayGGGo](https://github.com/SayGGGo). |
| What? | Learning, practice, progress tracking and AI tools in one workspace. |
| Where? | [isvelocity.ru](https://isvelocity.ru/) and [vlty.ru](https://vlty.ru/). |
| When? | During exam preparation, university exam periods, the start of a school year and project deadlines. |
| Why? | To keep context, make the next step clearer and turn knowledge into results. |
